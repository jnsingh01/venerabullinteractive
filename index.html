Connection

Forever

a Venerabull Interactive product

People form some of their deepest attachments with animals, and a photograph can only keep one moment of them. Forever is being built to keep the animal itself.

The aim
To recreate a person’s own pet from their own photos: not a breed, not a likeness of a breed, but that animal, with its identity holding steady across every view, in movement and through later corrections.
Where it stands
In development. Current work is on likeness. Not released, and no release date is set.

Red Button

from Venerabull Interactive

When you’re about to do something you might regret, you press it.

The idea
One luminous button for the moment before an impulse, when you aren’t quite thinking like yourself. It’s designed to ask less of you the less you have to give: almost nothing to read, type or decide while it’s in use.
Where it stands
In development as a native app, being built for iPhone first. Not released, and no release date is set.
